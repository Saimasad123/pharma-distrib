# 💊 PharmaDistrib

### AI-Powered Pharmaceutical Distribution Management SaaS

PharmaDistrib is a full-stack SaaS platform designed to help pharmaceutical distributors manage their day-to-day business operations from a centralized dashboard.

The platform helps businesses manage products, suppliers, customers, purchases, sales, inventory, revenue, and profit while providing analytics and insights to support better business decisions.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🔐 Authentication & Authorization
- User registration and login
- Secure authentication
- Role-based access control
- Protected routes and API endpoints
- Organization-based user management

### 📦 Product & Inventory Management
- Add, update, and delete products
- Track product quantities
- Monitor inventory levels
- Search and manage products

### 🏭 Supplier Management
- Manage pharmaceutical suppliers
- Maintain supplier information
- Track supplier-related purchases

### 👥 Customer Management
- Manage customer records
- Track customer transactions
- Maintain customer information

### 🛒 Purchase Management
- Record product purchases
- Track purchase costs
- Manage supplier purchases
- Update inventory automatically

### 💰 Sales & Profit Management
- Record product sales
- Calculate product-level profit
- Track total revenue
- Monitor monthly and yearly performance
- Analyze business profitability

### 📊 Dashboard & Analytics
- Business performance overview
- Revenue analytics
- Profit analytics
- Inventory statistics
- Sales insights

### 🤖 AI-Powered Features
- AI business assistant
- Natural-language business data analysis
- AI-powered inventory insights
- Sales and demand forecasting
- Intelligent business recommendations

> AI features are designed to help distributors analyze business data and make more informed decisions.
